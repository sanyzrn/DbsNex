# 31 · Whole-product audit and roadmap — Nex 2.6.23

> Status: **completed for 2.6.23.** Scope: the nineteen-part brief "comprehensive
> audit, modernization and product excellence" — architecture, performance,
> data, stability, security, UI, UX, modules, AI, languages, Android, tests,
> CI, dependencies, build, compatibility, repository health, documentation and
> product. Reviewed against `main` at `6de8e6b` (2.6.22 prepared, 2.6.21 the
> last published release), on the repository's own pin: Flutter 3.47.9.

This document is the answer to one question: *after many hands and many
releases, what state is Nex actually in, and what should change?* It records
what was verified, what was changed, what was measured and deliberately left
alone, and what is next. It does not repeat the findings of the reviews before
it — [`28`](./28-2.5.4-audit-triage.md), [`30`](./30-2.6.1-audit-triage.md),
[`development/technical-debt-review-2.6.17.md`](./development/technical-debt-review-2.6.17.md),
[`development/nex-2.6.19-performance.md`](./development/nex-2.6.19-performance.md) —
it checks whether they still hold.

## 1. The short answer

The worry behind the brief was that accumulated changes had left the codebase
inconsistent, duplicated, slow and fragile. **Measured, it is not.** The
boundaries the architecture document promises are enforced by CI rather than by
convention, every suite passes on the pinned toolchain, the hot paths were
re-engineered three releases ago with numbers attached, and the security
surface has been reviewed finding by finding. A rewrite of any layer would
trade verified behaviour for unverified behaviour and is not justified.

What the audit did find is of a different kind — things no code-level review
catches, because the code is doing exactly what it was written to do:

1. **Every launch waited two seconds on purpose** (§3.1). Fixed.
2. **The daily nudge fell back to generic lines** on the most common kind of
   day, a quiet one (§3.2). It now has something of the person's own to say.
3. **Compiled Python bytecode was committed** and a retired manual workflow was
   still listed (§3.3). Removed.
4. **The guide ships without pictures** and **the app targets API 35** — both
   need a real device and are the owner's next two actions (§6).

## 3. What changed in 2.6.23

### 3.1 The opening sequence is an introduction, not a toll

`bootstrap_host.dart` raced the real bootstrap against a two-second timer and
waited for whichever finished last — on every launch, including the capture
widget, the Quick Settings tile, a tapped reminder and the restart after a
restore. For an app whose first principle is that capture never waits, that
was the largest fixed latency in the product, and it was invisible to every
performance review because nothing was slow: the wait was a constant.

Now:

- The sequence plays **in full on the first launch of a version** (a fresh
  install, an update) and is remembered in `app.intro_seen`.
- **Every other launch has no floor.** The splash holds on the two eyes — the
  same picture Android's own splash leaves on screen — so a library that opens
  quickly is reached with nothing in between. If opening takes longer than
  `NexSplash.patience` (700 ms), the sequence starts, so a slow cold start still
  does not look frozen.
- With animations turned off, nothing moves in either case.

Covered by four new cases in `test/nex_splash_test.dart`.

Also removed: the export-cache sweep ran twice during the same bootstrap.

### 3.2 "On this day" in the daily nudge

From the roadmap's own ideas list (`11-roadmap-2.0.md` §5), in the form that
costs nothing at capture and adds no screen:

- `NexNudgeSnapshot.memories` — notes with words of their own (text, or a
  captioned photo or voice note) written around this time of year in each of
  the last five years; at most two a day, the longest first. Deleted and
  set-aside notes, checklists, links and files are never offered.
- When a planned day has **nothing due, urgent or unfinished**, its body is the
  memory — "A year ago today you wrote: …" — instead of the neutral rotation.
  Anything real always comes first; a memory only fills a quiet day.
- The anniversary is the date the person reads: with the Persian calendar it
  is the same day of the same Persian month, which the Gregorian date misses by
  a day in some years (tested with 12 Aban 1403/1404).
- Local only: no request is made, and the existing lock-screen secrecy of the
  nudge applies unchanged.
- Its own switch under the nudge (`nudge.memories`, on by default), because not
  every old note is one to meet again unasked.

Data cost: 95 day-sized range reads of `idx_notes_created_at`, pinned to that
index by name — the planner otherwise prefers `deleted_at IS NULL` on another
index and reads every live note once per day asked. A test asserts the plan.

### 3.3 Repository hygiene

- `tools/__pycache__/*.pyc` — fourteen compiled files — were tracked. Removed,
  and `__pycache__/` is ignored.
- `.github/workflows/polish-stage-check.yml` removed: a manual-only duplicate of
  the client CI job without `--fatal-infos`, named after a retired stage.
- A stale comment in `release.yml` said the in-app updater cannot know the
  device's ABI; it has matched the running ABI since the split APKs shipped.

## 4. Measured and deliberately left alone

- **`PRAGMA optimize` / `ANALYZE`.** The standard advice for a long-lived SQLite
  connection. Measured on a 20,000-note mixed library (best of 15, on disk, the
  shipped WAL configuration): planner statistics made nothing meaningfully
  faster, and the sampled statistics `PRAGMA optimize` produces made
  `tagUsage` twice as slow (8.6 → 15.9 ms) and the outbox scan sixty times
  slower (0.03 → 2.0 ms). The hand-built indexes already encode the right
  plans. **Not adopted.**
- **Splitting the large package files** (`ai_provider.dart`,
  `note_repository.dart`). Still the judgement of the 2.6.17 review: a metric,
  not a defect. Each has its behaviour suite; splitting them is churn in the two
  files with the most safety-critical logic.
- **A broader lint set, relative-import sweep, token migrations.** Same
  reasoning as 2.6.17 (D2, D4–D7): no user-visible or correctness benefit.
- **Schema-step short-circuit on open.** `_migrate` re-asserts the whole layout
  on every open. It is idempotent, and skipping it on a matching
  `user_version` would make the version stamp load-bearing for changes that
  today do not need to bump it. Worth revisiting only with a phone measurement
  that shows it matters (§6, item 4).

## 6. What is next

In order of value to the person using the app. Items marked **device** cannot
be finished from a workstation and are the owner's.

1. **Guide screenshots — device.** All 26 images under
   `apps/client/assets/guide/images/` are still generated placeholders. The
   guide hides them by hash, so nothing broken is shown — but the in-app guide
   has no pictures, and the placeholders are ~370 KB of dead weight in every
   APK. Capture the thirteen screens in both languages on the release build.
2. **Target API 36 — done in 2.6.26; device check outstanding.** `targetSdk`
   is 36. Each Android 16 change is met where it lands, and the reasoning sits
   beside `targetSdk` in `app/build.gradle.kts`:
   - *Fixed orientation ignored on large screens (600dp and up).* The portrait
     lock stays for phones. On tablets and unfolded foldables the phone layout
     is already adaptive: `large_screen_test.dart` lays out the timeline,
     capture, a note, first run and Settings at four tablet and foldable
     shapes in both languages, and holds the timeline to a centred reading
     width. The camera no longer locks capture upright there
     (`platform/large_screen.dart`), so a landscape shot is not saved sideways.
     The temporary compatibility opt-out Android 16 offers was deliberately not
     used: it disappears in Android 17.
   - *Predictive back for every activity.* Opted in since
     `enableOnBackInvokedCallback`; no native code uses `onBackPressed` or
     `KEYCODE_BACK`.
   - *No edge-to-edge opt-out.* The app has drawn edge to edge since API 35.
   - `android_16_target_test.dart` keeps all four declarations from drifting.

   **On a device before publishing:** a tablet or foldable in both
   orientations — rotate on the timeline, inside a note and inside capture;
   take a photo held landscape and check it is upright in the note; the back
   gesture's preview on a phone; the 2.6.19 manual checklist. No Android SDK
   was reachable from the workstation, so the build itself is proven by CI's
   Android job on the pull request.
3. **`flutter_local_notifications` 23 — done in 2.6.26; device check
   outstanding.** 22.3.3 → 23.0.2. Read change by change against what Nex
   uses:
   - The one breaking change that reaches Nex: the plugin compiles against
     API 37 and asks the app to. `compileSdk` is pinned to 37 (it only decides
     which APIs the build can see; `targetSdk` stays 36), and
     `tools/install_android_ndk.sh` now installs that platform with retries
     before Gradle runs, in CI, the release and the manual AI build — the same
     protection the NDK got after a truncated mid-build download in 2.1.6.
   - The exact-alarm call moved from `AlarmManagerCompat.setExact` to
     `AlarmManager.setExact`, which is what the compat call did on every API
     level Nex supports: no change in when a reminder fires.
   - Fixed: a scheduled notification serialized by an older plugin version
     could crash on delivery after an update for lack of an icon; cancelling a
     tag-based notification after an action that opens the app.
   - Background activity launches from the plugin's PendingIntents are
     explicitly denied on Android 14+. A notification tap is a user
     interaction and is not affected; Nex's actions that open the app go
     through the tap.
   - The receivers the manifest declares by class name still exist.
   - No Dart API Nex calls changed: analysis and the full suite pass
     unchanged.

   **On a device before publishing**, every alarm path once: an exact reminder
   on time; with exact alarms refused, the inexact fallback and its warning; a
   reminder set before a reboot arriving after it; one set on 2.6.25 arriving
   after updating to 2.6.26 (the case the icon fix is about); a recurring
   reminder; the daily nudge; a module reminder's Done action; all of it with
   the battery saver on.
4. **Measure the launch on a phone.** Settings → measuring records "cold start
   to library open". Compare 2.6.22 and 2.6.23 on a mid-range phone and put the
   two numbers in this file; if opening regularly exceeds 700 ms the next
   candidates are the awaited widget snapshot and reminder initialisation in
   `_start`.
5. **Clipboard capture offer.** From the ideas list. Only worth doing through
   the capture sheet's own paste affordance: reading the clipboard on open
   raises Android's "pasted from clipboard" notice on every launch, which is
   the opposite of quiet.
6. **Continuity (roadmap §3).** Unchanged: pairing first, then automatic sync.
   It remains the largest gap between Nex and the products it is compared with,
   and it is correctly gated on a second device being ready.
7. **One name for the nudge in Persian.** Settings says «یادآور روزانه», the
   guide and the notification channel say «یادآوری روزانه».
