# Brief: Family fund (`family_fund`) — «صندوق خانوادگی»

**Job:** New. There is no code.

## Who it is for

The person who runs a family's or colleagues' loan fund (صندوق قرض‌الحسنه
خانوادگی): every member pays a share each month, and each month (or
round) one member, drawn by lot, receives the pot as a loan to repay. Today
this is kept in a notebook or a spreadsheet, and the draw happens in a
group chat.

## Must have

- **The fund:** name, monthly share, start month, number of shares per
  member (some hold two), and its rules as free text.
- **Members:** name and shares; joining and leaving keep their history.
- **Monthly payments:** who has paid this month, who is late, and the
  fund's balance. Marking a payment takes one tap.
- **A fair draw:**
  - only members who have not yet won in this round are eligible, and the
    list is shown before drawing;
  - the result is kept for good, with the date and the eligible list;
  - a redraw is possible only with a written reason, and both results stay
    in the history.
- **Repayment:** the winner's loan as instalments, ticked as they are paid.
- **The month's status as text,** ready to send to the group: who paid, who
  is left, the balance, this month's winner.
- **Reminders** for the manager: the payment day, and the draw day.

## Must not

- Calculate or suggest interest, or frame anything as financial advice.
- Collect or move money, or reach anything over the network.

## Platform requests to expect

A monthly status *image* for the group, with the fund's name and a small
Nex mark: ask for image sharing, and share text meanwhile.

## Open questions to list in the README

This need has not been checked with real fund managers. List what you
