# Brief: Price Lens (`price_lens`) — «مقایسهٔ قیمت»

**Job:** Upgrade (Soon). The code exists; finish and upgrade it.

## Who it is for

A shopper standing in front of a shelf: is the 900 g pack or the 1.5 kg
pack cheaper per kilo? And at home: how much has this product gone up since
last time?

## Must have

- **Compare in seconds:** two or more options, each a price and a size;
  the cheapest per unit is marked and the difference said plainly ("the
  1.5 kg is 12% cheaper per kilo").
- **Units that make sense:** grams/kilos, millilitres/litres, count
  (pieces, rolls, sheets), with conversion between them.
- **Price memory per product:** save a price with its date and shop; the
  product shows its last prices and the change ("last time 45,000, now
  62,000: +38% in 3 months").
- **A shopping list view** of saved products with their last known price.
- **Share** a comparison or a product's price history as text.

## Iran

Tomans and rials; Persian digits in and out; sizes as written on Iranian
packaging.

## Must not

- Fetch prices or name shops the person did not write.
