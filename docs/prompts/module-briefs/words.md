# Brief: Word by Word, now for learning a language (`words`) — «واژه به واژه»

**Job:** Upgrade (live). People use this module today. Read the live rules
in prompt 12 twice: nothing saved may be lost or changed in meaning, and
nothing used today may disappear.

## Who it is for

Someone learning a language (most often English) who wants to keep and
really learn the words they meet, offline, in a few minutes a day.

## Must have

- **Everything Word by Word does today**, unchanged for someone who never
  touches the new parts.
- **Decks:** words grouped into collections (a book, a course, a trip); a
  word can stay outside any deck.
- **Practice that teaches,** from the person's own words:
  - flashcards with self-grading (the spaced review the module already has,
    audited and kept);
  - typing the answer, with forgiving matching (case, extra spaces, Persian
    and Arabic forms of ی and ک);
  - multiple choice, the wrong choices drawn from the same deck;
  - both directions (word → meaning, meaning → word).
- **Example sentences** per word, written by the person, shown in practice.
- **A small daily goal** ("10 reviews") and a quiet note of what is due
  today. **No streaks, no shaming,** nothing lost for a missed day.
- **Import a list** pasted as text, one word per line with its meaning after
  a separator (tab, dash, colon, `=`), with a preview before anything is
  saved.
- **Progress:** words learned, words due, how review went this week.
- **An optional daily reminder** for review, off by default.

## Must not

- Fetch dictionaries, translations or pronunciation.
- Claim fluency, levels or certificates.

## Platform requests to expect

Spoken pronunciation (text to speech); a learning card on the home screen;
a review exercise inside the notification. Ask for them; build without.
