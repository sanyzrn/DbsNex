# Brief: Lent & Borrowed, plus "Between us" (`lent_borrowed`) — «قرض و طلب» و «بین خودمان»

**Job:** Upgrade (live). People use this module today. Read the live rules
in prompt 12 twice: nothing saved may be lost or changed in meaning, and
nothing used today may disappear.

## Who it is for

Friends, flatmates, families and colleagues who share costs and settle up
by card-to-card transfer, and lose each other's card numbers in chats.

## Must have

- **Everything Lent & Borrowed does today,** unchanged.
- **Split a cost (دنگ):** an amount, who paid, who shares it — equally or by
  custom shares or amounts. Several costs make a "split" (a trip, a month in
  a shared flat).
- **Settle up with the fewest transfers:** the module works out who pays
  whom how much, and each transfer can become an ordinary loan record with
  one tap, so it shows in the lists that already exist.
- **A message per person,** ready to send, in the style of the module's
  existing messages: what they owe, to whom, and the card or IBAN to pay to.
- **A book of cards and IBANs** for the people in the module: card number
  (16 digits, Luhn-checked), IBAN (شبا, `IR` + 24 digits, mod-97 checked),
  the account holder's name, and the bank recognised offline from the card's
  first six digits (a table in the module, marked with the date it was
  checked). Copy in one tap, digits grouped, Persian or English digits.

## Iran

Card-to-card culture; IBAN in the Iranian format; bank names in Persian;
tomans and rials as the module already shows them.

## Must not

- Store a CVV2, an expiry date, a PIN or any password. Only the card number
  and the IBAN, which people share openly to be paid.
- Move money, open bank apps by deep link, or fetch anything.

## Platform requests to expect

Recognising a 16-digit card number written in an ordinary note and offering
"save as Ali's card" lives on the timeline, outside this module: ask for it.
