# Brief: Parking (`parking`) — «پارکینگ»

**Job:** Upgrade (Soon). The code exists; finish and upgrade it.

## Who it is for

Someone who parks in a big car park, a mall or a street with paid parking,
and wants to find the car and not overstay.

## Must have

- **"I parked" in one tap,** with what is easiest at that moment: floor,
  spot, a short clue ("by the blue pillar, near lift B"). Everything
  optional.
- **Paid until:** a time, with a reminder some minutes before (default 10,
  editable), and the time left shown large while it runs.
- **"I'm back":** ends the parking, and keeps it in a short history.
- **Favourite places** (home, work, the usual mall) to fill the clue in one
  tap.
- **One-handed and fast:** the main screen works with a thumb, in a car
  park, in a hurry.

## Must not

- Ask for location or the camera — the API has neither. Ask for GPS and a
  photo of the spot under Platform requests, and work well without them.

## Later (describe in the README, do not build)

A "where's my car" card on the home screen while a parking is active.
