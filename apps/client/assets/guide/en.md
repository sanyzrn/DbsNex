# Using Nex

Nex is for catching a thought before it gets away. Everything else in here is
optional.

## Writing something down

Tap the **+** button. Start typing. That's it — there's no title to fill in and
no Save button; the note is yours the moment you close the sheet.

The same button gives you the other kinds of note:

- **Checklist** — a list you can tick off.
- **Voice** — hold and talk. Nex keeps the recording.
- **Photo** — take one or pick one, crop it, draw on it if you like.
- **File** — anything from your phone.
- **Link** — paste a URL and Nex fetches the page's name.

You can also share into Nex from any other app, and there's a home-screen
widget that opens straight into a new note.

## Making text look right

Select a word or a line while writing, and the menu that offers Cut and Copy
also offers **Bold**, *Italic*, `Mono`, ~~Strikethrough~~, Quote and Link.
**Regular** takes it all back off.

Formatting shows as you typed it while you're writing and rendered when you
read the note. A note without any is left exactly as written.

Tap a `mono` word in an open note to copy it.

## Files you share in

Nex shows a file rather than just naming it:

- Text, Markdown, spreadsheets (`.csv`), source and config files — read and
  laid out properly.
- Word documents (`.docx`) — headings, lists, tables and links all survive.
- PDFs — the first page. Tap it to open the file properly.
- Video — a frame of it. Tap to play it in your video player.
- Music — plays in the note.
- Pictures — shown full width, tap to expand.

Whatever Nex can't show, it still names, and one tap hands it to whatever app
does open it.

## Finding things again

The search box at the top of the home screen looks inside everything — your
words, what a voice note said, what a photo had written on it.

**Tags** are the other way through. Add them from a note, then tap one in the
row under the search box to see only those notes.

## Keeping a note in front of you

Cards show two lines. If one note needs to stay readable — a checklist you're
working through — open it and choose **Show in full**. That card grows to fit
all of it and stays that way. **Pin** does the other thing: it holds a note at
the top of the list. You can pin five.

## Reminders

Open a note, tap the bell, pick a time. Nex will bring it back.

A reminder can repeat — daily, weekly, whatever suits. A one-off clears itself
once it has rung, so it doesn't sit there looking overdue forever.

There's also a **daily nudge** in Settings → Notifications: one quiet
notification at a time you choose.

## The intelligence layer

This is entirely optional and off until you turn it on.

**Settings → Intelligence** is where you choose a provider — Anthropic,
OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or any service that speaks one of those formats.
Paste your API key, pick a model, and press **Test connection** to be sure it
works before you rely on it.

Your key is kept in the phone's own secure storage, not in a settings file.

With a provider connected, Nex can suggest tags, summarise a note, transcribe a
voice note, read text out of a photo, find related notes, and answer questions
about your library in the **Assistant**.

You can also set the language answers come back in, separately from the app's
own language — useful if you write in Persian and want summaries in Persian
regardless of the interface.

If you'd rather nothing left your phone at all, there's an on-device model you
can download instead.

## How Nex looks

**Settings → Appearance**:

- **Theme** — light, dark, or follow the system.
- **Accent colour** — pick the one you like.
- **Background** — plain, or one of three quiet patterns.
- **Liquid glass** — frosted surfaces, if your phone is up to it.
- **Text size** and **comfort mode** — for when the default is too tight.
- **Language** — English, Persian, or whatever your phone is set to.

## Capture and gestures

**Settings → Capture** decides how writing behaves: whether Enter sends the
note or starts a new line, and what a **swipe** on a card does. Set the left
and right edges to whichever two actions you reach for most.

## Locking Nex

**Settings → Security** puts your fingerprint, face or device passcode in front
of the app. It locks whenever Nex goes to the background.

## Your notes are yours

**Settings → Data and backup**:

- **Export** — everything, as a file you keep.
- **Import** — bring notes in from another app, or from a Nex export.
- **Backups** — Nex keeps its own, quietly.
- **Recently deleted** — a deleted note waits here before it's really gone.

Nex works with no account and no server. Sync exists if you want it and does
nothing until you set one up.

## Updates

Nex checks for a new version once a day and puts a dot on the settings icon
when there is one. Downloads keep going while you use the app, show up in the
notification shade, and if one is interrupted you can pick it up where it
stopped rather than starting again.

## If something looks wrong

**Settings → About** has the version number and a way to send feedback. The
changelog tells you what each release changed.
